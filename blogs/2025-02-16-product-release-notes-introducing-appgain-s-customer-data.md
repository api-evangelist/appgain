🔹 Overview We’re excited to announce the launch of Appgain’s Customer Data Platform (CDP) —a powerful event-driven data model designed to help businesses track, analyze, and automate user engagement . With real-time event tracking, comprehensive user profiles, and AI-powered automation , Appgain’s CDP enables businesses to optimize marketing campaigns, boost customer retention, and increase conversions . This release provides a unified view of user behavior across mobile apps, websites, and marketing channels, making it easier than ever to turn customer data into action.
