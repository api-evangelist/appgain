🆕 New Vendor Integration: Route Mobile We’re excited to announce that Appgain is now fully integrated with Route Mobile , a new WhatsApp Business API provider. This gives our users even more flexibility and better message delivery across global markets. ✅ What’s Included: ✅ Full support for sending and receiving WhatsApp messages via Route Mobile ✅ Faster approval and onboarding for new phone numbers ✅ Improved delivery rates for GCC, Africa, and Asia 🟢 New Feature: OBA (Official Business Account) Service We’ve also launched a Green Tick Verification (OBA) service in partnership with Route Mob
