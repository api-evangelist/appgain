## Products

### AI Agents
- **AI Customer Bot**: RAG-powered chatbot for customer support across WhatsApp, web, and mobile — with human handoff.
- **AI Call Intelligence**: Real-time call transcription, sentiment analysis, and action-item extraction.
- **Visual Bot Builder**: Drag-and-drop flow editor for building conversational bots without code.

### Channels
- **WhatsApp Business API**: Send personalized campaigns, automate support, and broadcast at scale via WhatsApp.
- **Email Marketing**: Drag-and-drop email builder with automation, warm-up, and deliverability tools.
- **SMS Marketing**: High-conversion SMS campaigns with multi-vendor routing.
- **Rich Push Notifications**: Video, carousel, and interactive push formats for mobile apps.
- **Web Push Notifications**: Browser-based push for visitor re-engagement.
- **Broadcast**: Bulk messaging across WhatsApp, SMS, email, and push from one dashboard.

### Orchestration
- **Marketing Automation**: Visual workflow builder for multi-channel automated campaigns.
- **Sequences**: Multi-step drip campaigns for onboarding, nurturing, and re-engagement.
- **Omnichannel Messaging**: Unified campaign management across all channels with behavioral triggers.
- **Unified Inbox**: Single inbox for all customer conversations across WhatsApp, email, SMS, and chat.

### Data & Insights
- **Customer Data Platform (CDP)**: Unified customer profiles, event tracking, and audience segmentation.
- **Marketing CRM**: Customer relationship management with behavioral segmentation and targeting.
